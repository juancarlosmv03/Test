The gym

Three floors of training

Everything you need for general fitness, serious training and body building, in a fully air-conditioned building.

Weights & machines

An extensive gym with a great range of free weights, resistance machines and multi-stations.

Cardio

All-new cardio equipment on the gym floor.

Boxing area & ring

A dedicated boxing area with its own ring, home of the Quesada Fitness Gym Boxing and MMA Club.

Three studios

State-of-the-art studios and dance areas where the group classes take place.

Sauna & changing rooms

Large shower facilities, a sauna and changing rooms.

Chill-out room & juice bar

Relax in the chill-out room. The juice bar is open after morning classes, and there is a shop on site.
